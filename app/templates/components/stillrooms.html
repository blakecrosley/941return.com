The Music room

Still Rooms, made for sitting.

An album written and recorded for this app. Ten pieces play in order while you sit, and the bell still comes when it is time. Every piece is a 5.1 mix, so on AirPods with Spatial Audio the music sits around you rather than in front of you. On Apple Watch it plays through your paired headphones, with previous and next beside pause and nothing else on the screen.

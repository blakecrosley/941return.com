iPhone Duo

Made for iPhone Duo.

The screen is one immersive canvas. Nothing on it scrolls, and there are two compositions, never a third.

Closed
The phone layout as it is. Theme, Health and Settings sit on the rail beside the Duo's vertical status bar, and the play button keeps the centre line.
Open
The digits grow and the four controls share one line. The room fills the display edge to edge.
Folded
A fold adds no layout, it confines the pieces. Book pose: timer and controls together in the trailing half, the scene in the leading. Table pose: timer above the fold, controls below, where the hands are.
